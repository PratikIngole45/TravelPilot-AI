# Goa Travel Overview

Goa is India's smallest state by area, located along the Konkan coast facing the Arabian Sea. It is internationally renowned for its golden-sand beaches, lush Western Ghats hinterland, and rich Indo-Portuguese heritage spanning over 450 years.

## Seasons and Weather
- **Peak Season (November to February):** Pleasant, dry weather with daytime temperatures around 28°C to 32°C. Ideal for sightseeing, water sports, and beach life.
- **Shoulder Season (October, March to April):** Warmer with fewer tourists and reasonable lodging rates.
- **Monsoon Season (June to September):** Heavy tropical rains transform the landscape into lush green; Dudhsagar Falls and wildlife sanctuaries are spectacular, while beach water sports are closed for safety.

## Transportation within Goa
- **Local Taxis & GoaMiles:** App-based GoaMiles operates across major transit points (Dabolim Airport, Mopa Airport, and Madgaon/Thivim railway stations).
- **Self-drive & Two-wheeler Rentals:** Scooters (approx. ₹300-₹500/day) and hatchbacks (approx. ₹1,200-₹1,800/day) are the most popular choices for local mobility. Fuel is extra.
- **Pilot Motorcycle Taxis:** Unique local motorcycle taxis ("pilots") operate for single passengers across cities like Panaji and Margao.

## Budget Planning Guidance
Daily local travel budgets (excluding intercity flights/trains) typically range from:
- Budget travelers: ₹1,500 - ₹2,500 per person per day (hostels/guesthouses, public buses/scooters, beach shacks).
- Mid-range travelers: ₹3,500 - ₹6,000 per person per day (boutique hotels, private car rentals, sit-down restaurants).
- Luxury travelers: ₹10,000+ per person per day (5-star beachfront resorts, fine dining, private yachts).

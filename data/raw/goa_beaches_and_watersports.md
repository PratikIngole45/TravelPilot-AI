# Goa Beaches & Coastal Guide

Goa is divided into two administrative districts—North Goa and South Goa—each offering markedly different coastal atmospheres.

## North Goa Beaches: Energetic and Social
North Goa is famous for its vibrant nightlife, water sports, bustling flea markets, and lively beach shacks:
- **Calangute & Baga:** Highly popular for family travel and group water sports (parasailing, jet-skiing, banana boat rides). Parasailing typically costs between ₹800 and ₹1,500 per person depending on season and duration.
- **Anjuna Beach:** Known for dramatic red laterite cliff backdrops, bohemian beach shacks (Curlies, Cafe Lilliput), and the Wednesday Anjuna Flea Market offering handicrafts, spices, and clothing.
- **Ashwem & Mandrem:** Calmer, wider stretches in northern Pernem with eco-chic resorts, yoga retreats, and shallow swimming waters.

## South Goa Beaches: Serene and Unspoiled
South Goa is ideal for relaxed vacations, couples, and nature lovers seeking quiet shorelines:
- **Palolem Beach:** A pristine crescent-shaped bay lined with leaning coconut palms and colorful wooden beach huts. Calm, protected waters make it safe for swimming and sea kayaking. Kayak rentals cost roughly ₹200 to ₹300 per hour.
- **Agonda Beach:** A tranquil, wide open beach designated as a protected nesting ground for Olive Ridley sea turtles. Motorized water sports and late-night loudspeakers are strictly prohibited to protect marine fauna.
- **Colva & Benaulim:** Long stretches of silvery sand popular with families, renowned for fresh seafood shacks operated by local fishing communities.

## Beach Safety Notice
- Red flags indicate hazardous rip currents; never swim where red flags are posted.
- Lifeguards (Drishti Marine) are stationed on major beaches from 7:30 AM to sunset.
